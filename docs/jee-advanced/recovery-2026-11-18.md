Recovered 989 previously absent printed keys. Each automated match requires a unique question, exact normalized native text, and complete matching mathematical glyph sequences for the stem and every ordered option. Explicit letters, multiple letters, numbers and inclusive ranges are supported. Written responses, ambiguous option ordering, unrecognized glyphs, and disjoint numerical alternatives are not guessed. A key is evidence of the printed answer, not independent academic verification. English recovery does not silently replace Hindi keys.

Six published/PDF answer disagreements were withheld. For example, the 2025 Paper 1 spherical-cavity question has two separate accepted numerical values in the official PDF; it is still review-only because a continuous range would incorrectly accept intermediate values.

Recovered 16 diagrams across nine 2025 Paper 1 questions from original official-PDF page regions. These are rendered crops preserving the drawing and labels, not redraws or watermark erasure. They passed page-context, visual, OCR and enhanced-contrast checks. Some contain DekhoExams branding from the supplied PDF; no competitor branding was observed. Unverified chapter-PDF artwork remains blocked.
