ScheduledOutboxPollerTest adds four checks. One uses Spring's ThreadPoolTaskScheduler to poll a committed H2 event on a background thread, call a local consumer, acknowledge the row, cancel the scheduled future and shut the scheduler down. A second lets a fake transport fail once, stores a future due time, then confirms the same event is delivered by a later tick. A third checks that a two-row batch leaves the third row pending until another poll. A fourth pre-commits the consumer mutation, leaves the outbox pending and verifies that a later duplicate-safe poll acknowledges without changing stock twice. The fixture has a bounded latch wait. ConcurrentOutboxLeaseTest adds a two-thread, two-connection H2 claim race and token-checked lease renewal using logical time. One conditional claim wins; stale or expired workers cannot renew. These fixtures are test-scoped and are not installed in ReceiptApplication.
